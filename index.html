03The full arc

One transition, cared for at every stage.

Perimenopause, menopause, and the years after are one continuous arc, not separate problems. Wherever you are on it, your care starts with where you are.

Cycles shift, hormones fluctuate, and many women are told their bloodwork is normal while everything feels different. It usually is not nothing, and this is the stage where measuring early pays off.

09Questions

Good to know.

What happens at the free consult?

A 30-minute conversation with our clinical team, in person in San Francisco or Palo Alto, or by phone. We review your symptoms, health history, and goals, then map out which options make sense for you. You leave with a clear plan, whether or not you book anything.

I am in perimenopause, or already past menopause. Is this for me?

Yes. We care for women across the whole transition: perimenopause (the years of change that can begin in your late 30s or 40s), menopause, and the years after. Wherever you are, the consult starts with where you are.

Do I have to start hormone therapy?

No. The consult is about understanding your body and your options. Hormone therapy is one path we may discuss, but only if it is right for you. Many plans combine several approaches, and some do not involve hormones at all.

Is hormone therapy safe?

Hormone therapy is a well-established medical option, but it is not right for everyone. Our physicians review your full history and labs, walk you through the benefits and risks for your situation, and monitor you over time. Everything is personalized and physician-supervised.

Is it really free?

Yes. The menopause consultation is complimentary (usually $100). No credit card is required to claim a spot, and there is no obligation.

Who will I be working with?

Biohackr Health is led by board-certified physicians, including OB/GYN Dr. Lori Bluvas, and staffed by licensed, experienced registered nurses. Every plan runs under physician supervision.

Where are you located?

San Francisco, 1877 Union Street in the Marina, and Palo Alto, 540 Bryant Street. Phone consults are available if visiting is not convenient.
